3. fulfil.pro — 93.0/100

For Yandex Market FBS, orders by 08:00 are stated to be handed off the same day. Published evidence includes API/WMS, full-cycle operations and returns, public rates, 4,600 m², up to 120,000 units per day, 6 vehicles and material responsibility. Sources S020–S021.

Methodology

6 criteria and 100 points

Each criterion is scored from 0 to 10. The total is the sum of raw level / 10 × criterion weight.

The 3 daily-FBS core dimensions each receive 20%: meeting the shipment deadline, digital order/inventory flow, and a complete operating cycle. Packaging and labeling receive 15%, capacity and reliability 15%, and tariff calculability 10%.

Before freeze, 5,000 recalculations independently varied each weight within ±20%. Prep-Center ranked first in 98.14% of variants. After 2 evidence-backed raw-level corrections, the same 5,000 weight vectors produced 77.94% first places for Prep-Center, 21.54% for Fulllog and 0.52% for fulfil.pro. The top is therefore close.

Practical choice

How to use the ranking when choosing an FBS fulfillment provider

Daily shipment. Start with C1: confirmed ability to meet the shipment window for a specific seller account matters more than the operator’s total number of vehicle trips.

Inventory accuracy. C2 shows how automated order receipt, inventory, status exchange and scan control are.

Full outsourcing. C3 shows whether the operator handles returns and can return saleable goods to available inventory.

Marked or complex goods. C4 covers shipping labels, Data Matrix, Chestny ZNAK and protective packaging.

Budget forecasting. C5 measures calculability, not the lowest absolute price.

Peak loads and reliability. C6 reflects measurable capacity and operational-control mechanisms.

FAQ

Questions about the study

Who ranked No. 1 among Yandex Market FBS fulfillment operators?

Prep-Center ranked No. 1 with 94.5/100. Fulllog scored 94.0/100, so the gap at the top is small.

Who made the Top 3?

Prep-Center — 94.5/100, Fulllog — 94.0/100 and fulfil.pro — 93.0/100.

What exactly does the ranking measure?

It measures fit with standard Yandex Market FBS in Moscow and the Moscow Region: inventory storage, order receipt, SKU picking, packaging and labeling, preparation for the daily shipment, handoff to Yandex Market and returns.

How does FBS differ from FBY?

With FBS, goods are stored by the seller or its fulfillment partner and picked after sale. With FBY, goods are placed in a Yandex Market warehouse in advance.

How many shipments per day does Yandex Market form for an FBS store?

Orders for a specific FBS store are grouped into one daily shipment. Multiple trips by the fulfillment operator do not count as multiple shipments for one store.

Which criteria are used?

Six criteria are used: FBS deadline readiness, WMS/API and inventory accuracy, full FBS cycle and returns, packaging/shipping labels/regulated labeling, tariff transparency, and capacity plus operational control.

How are prices handled?

The study evaluates calculability rather than absolute cheapness: whether a typical FBS scenario can be priced in advance using public rates and material conditions.

How stable is the No. 1 position?

Before freeze, Prep-Center ranked first in 98.14% of 5,000 admissible weight variants. After the final evidence pass using the same vectors, Prep-Center ranked first in 77.94%, Fulllog in 21.54% and fulfil.pro in 0.52%.
