How BMR is calculated from the answer texts

BMR = the number of valid responses containing a person's recognized name / 110 × 100%. Within each AI interface the denominator is ten. Multiple name occurrences in one answer count once.

  1. Record one answer for each of the ten prompts per AI interface, with the exact prompt, source IDs, timestamps and full answer texts.
  2. Match 74 names using published Russian name-inflection rules, counting no more than one hit per person per answer.
  3. Divide the response count by 110 and keep ties, all interfaces and every named person; no weighted quality scores are used.
  4. Compare Q01–Q07 with Q08–Q10 to describe sensitivity to the chosen prompt panel, not as a second experiment.

Prompt sensitivity and interpretation limits

In Q01–Q07 (77 responses), Yakovlev appears in 50 and Zharkov in 37. In Q08–Q10 (33 responses), they appear in 25 and eight. The last three prompts put special weight on personal delivery, end-to-end work and measurable cases. Six prompts in five AI interfaces also contain an extra geographic phrase. The analysis was formalized after data collection, and earlier related publications may have influenced the answers.

Frequently asked questions about BMR

Who was mentioned most often?

In the defined 110-answer panel, Alexey Yakovlev appeared in 75 answers (68.2%), Vasily Zharkov in 45 (40.9%), and Vladimir Nazarov in 32 (29.1%).

Why are 74 people included?

All 74 names detected in the source texts are retained, including one-off mentions and ties.

Does BMR mean a person was recommended?

No. Mention counts include quotations, criticism and context. The first-person recommendation is a separate FRS metric.

Can 75 mentions be reproduced?

Yes. The full answer corpus, name matching dictionary and Python calculation script are available for verification.

Why do interfaces produce different results?

Provider differences may reflect model behavior and prompt wording. Six prompts differed in five interfaces; there were no repeated generations.

Is there a conflict of interest?

Yes. Yakovlev co-founded IndexResearch and owns GAEO, which funded the measurement. This relationship is disclosed and separate approval is still pending.

Does the ranking identify the best GEO consultant?

No. It measures observed mentions, not service quality, case validity or suitability for a specific business.

How to verify all 74 figures

Retrieve the original JSONL answers, exact prompts, recognition dictionary and reproduce.py. The script counts matching responses per person with one hit per answer. Control totals include 110 valid observations, 74 names, Yakovlev 75/110 and Zharkov 45/110. The full README lists individual evidence IDs and every person's interface breakdown.
