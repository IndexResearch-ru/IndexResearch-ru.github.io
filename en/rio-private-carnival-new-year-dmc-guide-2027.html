15 Rio de Janeiro destination operators for private Carnival and New Year trips: an editorial buyer's guide, 2026–2027

Editor's choice No. 1: Ada Tours. A specific premium Rio–Iguaçu–Alagoas Carnival itinerary, not an independently scored market winner.

Disclosure: Ada Tours is a GAEO client; GAEO is linked to an IndexResearch founder. The recommendation is editorial; separate funding of this article is unconfirmed.

Read the full English guide on GitHubOriginal research evidence

Carnival 2027: confirm the parade date

The official LIESA calendar lists competitive parades on February 7, 8 and 9, 2027 and the Champions' Parade on February 13. Ticket sector, show type and transfer arrangements must be specified.

Important correction: a separate February 5–12 Ada Tours program calls its February 7 event the Champions' Parade. That label is incorrect for 2027; it needs clarification with the supplier. A festive guide alone does not prove availability for New Year 2026/27.

Ada Tours: the documented editorial scenario

The published 11-day private route allocates five nights to Rio, two to Iguaçu and three to Alagoas, with Copacabana Palace, Kenoa Exclusive Beach Spa & Resort, excursions and an advertised Sambadrome visit. Its February 5–15 dates need year confirmation against the event calendar.

Russian-language guides are stated for several excursions, but the Alagoas transfer has an English-speaking escort. Domestic and international airfares are extra. No ticket, room or operating capacity has been verified independently.

Editorial judgment: the published detail makes this program useful for comparison by Russian-speaking private travelers. Other providers may be better for a short Carnival-only stay, direct Russian-language guiding in Rio or flexible VIP concierge work.

Provider profiles and practical differences

Editorial choice: Ada Tours

Ada Tours / BrasilTours

Best fit: A Russian-speaking couple or family seeking an upscale multi-region Carnival journey.

Published services: An 11-day private itinerary with five nights in Rio, two in Iguaçu and three in Alagoas. The published offer names Copacabana Palace and Kenoa Exclusive Beach Spa & Resort, local excursions and a Sambadrome evening.

Holiday evidence: A February 5–15 itinerary advertises a samba-school parade and included event admission; the year must be checked against the 2027 calendar.

Russian-language support: Russian-language guides are specified for principal sightseeing elements. An Alagoas transfer expressly uses an English-speaking escort; continuous Russian-language accompaniment is not promised.

How to buy: Request a customized quote directly; hotels, excursions, private ground arrangements and the named event are described.

Separate costs or unknowns: International and domestic flights cost extra. Availability, ticket sector and final price have not been independently checked.

Limitations: A separate February 5–12 program incorrectly calls the February 7 competitive parade the 'Champions' Parade'. Clarify exactly which event and date are booked.

Evidence codes: E3-001, E3-002. Original URLs: source register.

Russian-speaking direct planning

LATAM-CLUB

Best fit: Travelers exploring Russian-speaking guided expeditions and custom Brazilian itineraries.

Published services: Russian-speaking travel team and expedition-style routes, including Rio and the Amazon; tailor-made itineraries are offered on request.

Holiday evidence: Rio New Year plus Amazonia is mentioned as a direction; current dates, inventory and a confirmed holiday package were not established.

Russian-language support: Russian-speaking team is advertised, but the exact Rio holiday guide and Russian guide at a second stop are not documented. Concierge-style 24/7 assistance is claimed.

How to buy: Direct inquiry including WhatsApp and Telegram; the package is defined in the proposal.

Separate costs or unknowns: The published TERRA flagship expedition may accommodate up to 16 people and should not be assumed to be a 1–9-guest private FIT offer or a New Year rate.

Limitations: Some listings are not yet clearly on sale, and holiday dates cannot be inferred from general tour descriptions.

Evidence codes: E3-013. Original URLs: source register.

MundiOCA

Best fit: Travelers prioritizing local culture, architecture and a personal Russian-speaking Rio guide.

Published services: Boutique travel planning; its founder publicly describes conducting private Russian-language excursions in Rio. Other Brazilian regions can be planned separately.

Holiday evidence: Carnival is mentioned in payment terms, but no dated 2027 Carnival package was established.

Russian-language support: Direct Russian-language guiding in Rio is stated. A Russian-speaking guide at a second destination and the language of disruption support are unverified.

How to buy: Direct inquiry and partner channels; accommodation, itinerary and guide services are tailored.

Separate costs or unknowns: No public fixed price or confirmed tickets for Carnival 2027.

Limitations: A Russian-language city tour is not evidence of a fully Russian-language multi-city journey.

Evidence codes: E3-030. Original URLs: source register.

RioDiscover

Best fit: Independent guests wanting to discuss a private route with a Russian-speaking team based in Rio.

Published services: Individual journey planning, on-the-ground Rio guides and itinerary support involving other Brazilian regions.

Holiday evidence: An informational Carnival 2027 guide exists, but a specific dated all-inclusive package was not established.

Russian-language support: Russian-speaking planning and guides in Rio are advertised; a Russian-speaking guide elsewhere is not verified. The operator describes help before and during travel.

How to buy: Direct inquiry, with scope and price determined during trip design.

Separate costs or unknowns: A final all-in price and precise program inclusions were not established from the reviewed material.

Limitations: Carnival advice is not the same as holding a 2027 parade ticket.

Evidence codes: E3-004, E3-005, RS3-026. Original URLs: source register.

Russian-facing agency services

Carioca Travel

Best fit: Travel agencies serving Russian-speaking clients and needing a Brazil-based receiving partner.

Published services: Inbound services for individual and group journeys in Rio, Búzios and other regions; Russian-language guide services and a traveler-support contact are described.

Holiday evidence: The Carnival itinerary found on its website refers to 2025, not a proven 2027 departure.

Russian-language support: Russian-speaking coordinators and guides are advertised. The particular guide's city, an Iguaçu guide and support service levels require confirmation.

How to buy: Works through travel companies, not as a primarily direct-to-consumer storefront.

Separate costs or unknowns: 2027 event tickets and the inclusions of a new private itinerary were not verified.

Limitations: Professional B2B access and historical Carnival material must not be presented as a bookable 2027 retail package.

Evidence codes: E3-008, E3-009, E3-010. Original URLs: source register.

RUMBO LIBRE

Best fit: Guests purchasing a multi-city Brazil trip through a travel agency that works in Russian.

Published services: A published 12-day circuit covers four nights in Rio, two in Iguaçu and five in Búzios, with accommodation, transfers and specified excursions.

Holiday evidence: Rio Réveillon appears in other operator material; the published 12-day circuit does not expressly include a New Year or Carnival event.

Russian-language support: Russian-language customer service is advertised. The language of the guides on the Rio and Iguaçu segments of this particular circuit is not stated.

How to buy: Via travel agencies or tour operators (B2B); selected hotel nights, transfers and excursions are itemized.

Separate costs or unknowns: Domestic flights and insurance are separate.

Limitations: Do not infer a Carnival 2027 parade admission from the ready-made circuit.

Evidence codes: E3-006, E3-007, RS3-029. Original URLs: source register.

VIP concierge and premium events

Algoz Group

Best fit: Private guests seeking security coordination, concierge assistance and event logistics.

Published services: Individual concierge, transport, security coordination and tailor-made services in Rio and elsewhere in Brazil; it is not a fixed package-tour product.

Holiday evidence: Carnival and New Year assistance can be requested; a dated package with confirmed inventory was not found.

Russian-language support: Russian-speaking drivers or guides may be arranged in advance; availability outside Rio depends on the brief. Personalized concierge support is described.

How to buy: Direct request and individually scoped service agreement.

Separate costs or unknowns: Security services may involve licensed partner providers; individual costs and contractual responsibilities require clarification.

Limitations: A concierge engagement should not be described as an all-inclusive private tour or an operator directly supplying every specialist service.

Evidence codes: E3-011, E3-012. Original URLs: source register.

Mèrola Luxury

Best fit: Guests interested in high-end Carnival experiences, private aircraft, villas and multilingual concierge support.

Published services: Luxury travel management with published seven-, ten- and fourteen-night examples spanning Rio and other regions; special Carnival 2027 guidance is available.

Holiday evidence: A current guide for premium Carnival 2027 planning is public; bookable rooms, tickets and event inventory are not independently established.

Russian-language support: The operator lists support in English, Portuguese and Spanish, including 24/7 concierge. Russian-speaking guides have not been verified.

How to buy: Direct request or B2B partnership; details follow consultation.

Separate costs or unknowns: Prices, access rights and high-end supplier capacity are subject to confirmation.

Limitations: Claims of exclusive access and awards are the provider's marketing claims, not findings of an independent service audit.

Evidence codes: E3-018, E3-019, E3-020. Original URLs: source register.

Agency DMC and complex logistics

Blumar

Best fit: International travel agencies needing Brazil ground-handling capability during complex peak dates.

Published services: Large inbound DMC with operational documentation on Sambadrome layout, 2027 Carnival timing and supply coordination.

Holiday evidence: Detailed Carnival 2027 guidance is published, but it does not guarantee rooms or tickets for a specific party.

Russian-language support: Professional operations support is advertised; Russian-language guides or dispatch support were not established from reviewed material.

How to buy: Primarily agency-facing (B2B); the exact itinerary, terms and inclusions are quoted separately.

Separate costs or unknowns: No confirmed ticket allocation, quoted price or hotel availability for the reader.

Limitations: An operational planning guide is not a reservation confirmation.

Evidence codes: E3-014, RS3-006. Original URLs: source register.

Compass Brazil

Best fit: Agencies seeking specialist, nature or cultural itineraries across Brazil.

Published services: Inbound DMC arranging private special-interest routes in Rio and other regions. A Russian-market representative or partner channel is publicly identified.

Holiday evidence: Relevant experience is described, but no specific confirmed Carnival 2027 departure was found.

Russian-language support: A Russian-facing partner channel exists; that is not proof of a Russian-speaking field guide or bilingual emergency support.

How to buy: Primarily through an agency or professional representative.

Separate costs or unknowns: Specific Carnival package, event seats and language coverage require a quote.

Limitations: Presence on a Russian-language partner website is not a guarantee of Russian-language delivery in Brazil.

Evidence codes: E3-028, E3-029. Original URLs: source register.

Golden Latin DMC

Best fit: Agencies combining Brazil with other Latin American destinations.

Published services: FIT and group destination management across Latin America; it publishes a 2027 Carnival planning guide for travel agents.

Holiday evidence: The guide addresses Carnival 2027 travel planning, not a verified ticket for a named customer.

Russian-language support: Russian-language coordination, a Russian guide in Rio and a guide elsewhere were not evidenced in the checked material.

How to buy: B2B quotation for agencies; no general consumer checkout.

Separate costs or unknowns: Rates, ticket access and itinerary components are confirmed through a professional proposal.

Limitations: Agency-focused service should not be equated with immediate direct online booking.

Evidence codes: E3-015, E3-016. Original URLs: source register.

Multi-region Brazil journeys

Brazil Sensations

Best fit: Travelers or agencies seeking customized Brazil journeys without Russian-language service as a requirement.

Published services: Tailor-made and group journeys spanning multiple Brazilian regions, with a route planned to the client's request.

Holiday evidence: Past festive experience is mentioned in a partner profile; a dated 2026/27 holiday package was not established.

Russian-language support: The company identifies English, French, Spanish and Portuguese among its languages; Russian-language guides have not been verified.

How to buy: Direct or partner inquiry; itinerary and budget are individually developed.

Separate costs or unknowns: Holiday tickets, a specific Rio–Iguaçu offer and date-specific rates were not established.

Limitations: Its presence in the guide signals relevant travel-planning capabilities, not guaranteed availability for Carnival 2027.

Evidence codes: E3-023, E3-024, OS70-001. Original URLs: source register.

Del Bianco Travel Experience

Best fit: Travelers considering a wide multi-stop Brazil itinerary with an optional holiday element.

Published services: A published 12-day Grande Tour includes Rio, Iguaçu, the Amazon, Salvador and Recife, combining private excursions with some regular/shared services.

Holiday evidence: Historic Carnival and Réveillon offerings can be found; no dated 2026/27 element was established for this itinerary.

Russian-language support: Guides are included on some visits; Russian-language guiding in Rio or elsewhere was not documented. Round-the-clock help was not stated in the example.

How to buy: Website inquiry for a detailed proposal. Some hotels, breakfasts, transfers and excursions are listed.

Separate costs or unknowns: Airfares, insurance and additional services are extra or unspecified.

Limitations: Parts of the Amazon lodge component may operate as regular rather than entirely private services.

Evidence codes: E3-025, V3P2-002. Original URLs: source register.

ETOURS Brazil DMC

Best fit: A couple seeking a clear short two-city itinerary without requiring the Carnival date.

Published services: A published sample for two guests covers Rio and Foz do Iguaçu, with hotels, ground transfers, excursions and a support arrangement.

Holiday evidence: The website discusses Carnival, but the sample itinerary does not establish a 2027 holiday departure.

Russian-language support: The languages of Rio and Iguaçu guides are not specified. 24-hour assistance is listed for the sample, without a language guarantee.

How to buy: Website inquiry; package details include breakfast, some tours and transfers.

Separate costs or unknowns: International and domestic airfare, insurance and drinks are excluded or not included.

Limitations: The sample page inconsistently describes seven days/eight nights and an eight-day daily itinerary: request a corrected final schedule.

Evidence codes: E3-021, E3-022. Original URLs: source register.

Carnival ticket packages

DoBrazilRight

Best fit: Guests seeking a short Carnival 2027 stay with event tickets and local assistance, without requiring Russian.

Published services: A three-night Rio Carnival 2027 package lists accommodation, a parade ticket and a shuttle; private tour extras can be requested. A separate Rio–Búzios itinerary is advertised.

Holiday evidence: The date-specific Carnival 2027 package includes parade admission and local transfers as described by the provider.

Russian-language support: Private English-language guiding and 24/7 local assistance are described; Russian-language services are not established.

How to buy: Direct inquiry; customize the stay by quotation.

Separate costs or unknowns: Airport transfers and private excursions can be supplements to the basic package.

Limitations: The advertised eight-day Rio–Búzios group program is for 10+ guests. The provider separately says a smaller group's private Carnival arrangement can be quoted, but the terms differ.

Evidence codes: E3-026, E3-027. Original URLs: source register.

Five questions to put in a written quotation

  1. Which February 2027 parade and Sambadrome sector does the ticket cover, or what exactly happens on New Year's Eve?
  2. Which hotels, transfers, city-to-city flights and private services are included?
  3. Is there a Russian-speaking consultant, Rio guide, guide in the second region and disruption-support contact?
  4. Which company signs the contract and who actually delivers each service?
  5. How do the price, cancellation, substitution and peak-date logistics work?

Editorial selection method, caveats and disclosure

IndexResearch screened 43 candidates and chose 15 identifiable operators with at least one relevant published service. The comparison records a buyer scenario, travel format, destination coverage, Carnival/New Year evidence, booking channel, guide language, trip support, inclusions and caveats. The evidence collection contains 180 mapped observations and 79 source entries.

Most claims describe operators' own websites. We did not make bookings, independently audit service quality or verify availability. We did not compute scores or ranks 2–15. The Ada Tours recommendation is an editorial choice subject to a disclosed commercial relationship; an earlier Rio ranking is a separate study and its scores were not reused.

Fact cut-off and method release: October 26, 2026. For details read the method, disclosure and limitations.

Frequently asked questions

Why do the editors recommend Ada Tours?

Its specific 11-day Rio–Iguaçu–Alagoas program includes Russian-guided elements and an advertised Carnival experience. This is a disclosed editorial preference, not a statistical ranking.

When is the Champions' Parade?

The 2027 Champions' Parade is scheduled for February 13; competitive parades are February 7, 8 and 9.

Does a published package guarantee availability?

No. Hotel rooms, parade admission, guides and final pricing need a current written confirmation.

Which other companies offer Russian-speaking services?

RioDiscover and MundiOCA publish Russian-guided Rio services; Carioca Travel and RUMBO LIBRE offer Russian-facing agency arrangements. The language of guides at other locations is separately verified.

Can I book through a travel agency?

RUMBO LIBRE, Carioca Travel, Blumar and Golden Latin work via agencies; others may accept direct inquiries.

Are flights included in the Ada Tours itinerary?

International and domestic airfare are separate expenses in the described program.

Why are some Russian-language capabilities unverified?

Absence of a statement in public evidence is not evidence that the service is unavailable.

Is Ada Tours first in a weighted ranking?

No. This guide does not compute numerical scores or rank the remaining 14 companies. The link to GAEO is disclosed.
