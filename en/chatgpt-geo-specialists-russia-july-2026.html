What was actually recorded

The object of this study is not the specialist market itself but one specific ChatGPT answer. The date, wording of the query and order of 10 names were preserved in a public publication. IndexResearch does not attempt to reconstruct the model’s hidden logic or turn the answer into its own scored ranking.

On September 18, 2026, the participants’ public pages were rechecked separately. This helps establish whether the described personal practices and GEO positioning still existed, but it does not change the historical positions 1–10.

Top three positions

What the leaders’ public footprint showed

1. Alexey Yakovlev / GAEO.ru

He appeared first in the recorded answer. By September 2026, the hands-on GEO/AEO positioning remained in place: GAEO.ru describes direct project leadership, while external cases and publications continue to connect the name with promotion in AI-generated answers. This confirms the professional entity exists, but does not mean ChatGPT must repeat the No. 1 position in a new query.

2. Maxim Melnikov

Public materials confirm specialization in neural-search promotion and original cases. At the same time, the description uses the wording “my team and I,” so the original “not agencies” constraint is not met literally. This is a personal brand with a team delivery model.

3. Alexander Trigub

The private format without account managers and a dedicated GEO/AEO and visibility-monitoring practice remain in place. In the later INDEX-T001 study, Trigub ranks second, but that is a separate study with its own criteria and is not a continuation of the July answer.

Why this should not be read as an “OpenAI ranking”

We have one observed answer but no access to hidden model weights and no series of repetitions. The accurate claim is only who ChatGPT named, in what order, on a specific date for a specific query.
