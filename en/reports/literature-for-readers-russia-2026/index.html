Six criteria

C1 Close analysis of literary works30%
C2 Literary devices and textual form20%
C3 Historical context and intertextual links15%
C4 Learning sequence15%
C5 Independent exercises and understanding10%
C6 Access to the complete program10%

Each criterion is scored 0–4; ranges reflect unresolved evidence. Weights sum to 100%.

Program-by-program assessment

Synchronization («Синхронизация»): Books by Great Writers

An archived program describes analyses of 30+ literary works; the National Electronic Library collaboration describes 33 works across five periods.

Evidence limitation: The former course page returns 404, and the current full-course purchase path is unverified.

P01

Synchronization («Синхронизация»): Major Dystopian Novels

The recorded program covers several named dystopian novels and the ideas behind them.

Evidence limitation: Current availability of the complete recording remains provisional.

P02

Level One: Major Works of Russian Literature

The published program names six Russian works, lessons, exercises and quizzes.

Evidence limitation: An upcoming intake is evidence of registration, not completed teaching outcomes.

P04

Level One: Dostoevsky, Tolstoy, Chekhov

A course focused on works by three major Russian authors, with reading lists and short tasks.

Evidence limitation: Tasks are credited only to the extent documented for this specific product.

P05

Level One: Literature of England and America

The program covers writers and texts from British and American literary traditions.

Evidence limitation: Some available program descriptions are older and lesson counts require verification.

P06

Arzamas: How to Read Favourite Books Anew

An audio-led course offering alternative readings of familiar books and complementary materials.

Evidence limitation: Access to one lecture does not confirm access to the complete course.

P07

Arzamas: How Literature Works

The course introduces how literary forms and devices shape texts.

Evidence limitation: The full learning sequence and complete access terms are insufficiently documented.

P09

МИФ: Reading Room: Russian Classics

A structured program devoted to Russian classics; the cited full package distinguishes 18 core lessons from an introduction.

Evidence limitation: Lesson count alone cannot establish teaching quality.

P10

МИФ: Reading Room: World Classics

A literature program addressing several international traditions through a defined learning series.

Evidence limitation: Intake dates and paid-package details need a final current check.

P11

Правое полушарие Интроверта: History of Literature in 24 Hours

A recorded introductory survey of literary history for independent viewing.

Evidence limitation: Total duration does not itself indicate depth of analysis of individual works.

P12

Лекториум: Literature in Dialogue with Past and Present

A structured series exploring literature across time, with openly accessible lessons.

Evidence limitation: The course has an educational-professional emphasis that should remain visible in interpretation.

P14

Полка: KLIK Polka

An accessible collection of thematic readings and literary explanations.

Evidence limitation: A single course sequence and audience age need further confirmation.

P15

Полка: History of Russian Poetry

A specialized series on poetic tradition, development and historical context.

Evidence limitation: Its poetry focus limits generalization to all literary genres.

P16

Магистерия: Adventures of the American Dream

A recorded lecture series on US literature and cultural themes; earlier materials mention 12 related lectures.

Evidence limitation: Availability and composition of the complete course are not fully verified.

P17

Open Education / Saint Petersburg State University: Russian Literature in the Second Half of the 19th Century

An academically structured university course devoted to 19th-century Russian literature.

Evidence limitation: The official page mentions paid full access, but successful enrolment was not tested.

Practical selection advice

Compare the specific works discussed, explanations of literary technique, independent exercises and conditions for obtaining the full curriculum. Different products bearing the same title may not provide the same materials.

Frequently asked questions

Who is ranked first?

No final first place has been determined. The published numbers are diagnostic intervals, not approved rankings.

Why compare individual courses rather than schools?

Courses can differ substantially in content, exercises and access even within one provider.

Which Synchronization courses are included?

Books by Great Writers and Major Dystopian Novels are treated as separate programs.

Why is Books by Great Writers shown as 65–75?

Its C6 access criterion is unresolved and carries 10% of the frozen scoring model.

What do E, P and U mean?

E denotes an accepted source observation, P a provisional one, and U an unresolved property.

Were closed lessons and learning outcomes assessed?

No. The study compares publicly evidenced program features.

Did AI recommendations affect the scores?

No. Visibility in generative AI responses was not scored.

Is there an affiliate relationship?

Yes. Maria Yakovleva may receive a commission via Admitad for Synchronization.
