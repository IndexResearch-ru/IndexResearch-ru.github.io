Research profile

What was compared

A business delegation is more complex than a conventional corporate trip. Beyond flights and accommodation, it may require meetings with companies, visits to plants, interpreters, transport between sites, meeting rooms, events and a local team able to change the program without losing the business purpose.

The study therefore rewards business programs and technical visits more strongly than creative incentive programming by itself. A large conference or a MICE award does not guarantee a high score if company meetings and technical visits are weakly documented.

Leaders

Why the top three are ordered this way

1. Ada Tours · 96/100

A dedicated business-travel page publicly states company meetings, interpreters, transport, hotels and coordination. A separate MICE section adds venues, congresses, events and multilingual coordinators. The strength is the combination of a business agenda with local operational work. The limitation is a smaller public corpus of large international congresses than the largest MICE networks have.

2. Elcotour · 94/100

Elcotour has operated as a DMC since 1983 and separately describes technical groups: aviation manufacturers, agricultural trips, architectural and special visits. Multilingual guides, a supplier network, large sporting events and 24/7 IT are also documented. In this research question, direct technical visits give it a small advantage over Havas.

3. Havas Creative Tours · 94/100

Havas has a very strong MICE/DMC profile: operations in Brazil since 1989, congresses and meetings since 1994, a multilingual team and a long list of projects from 2025–2026. In a general MICE scenario it could rank no lower than Elcotour, but the current model rewards direct business programs slightly more.

Why Brazil Sensations is not higher

The independent Brazil's Best MICE Organiser 2024–2025 award strengthens the evidence base, but it does not produce the maximum score by itself: company meetings and technical visits matter more here.

Practical selection

What to verify before confirming a delegation

Who builds the business agenda?

Ask who identifies companies, arranges meetings and understands the purpose of technical visits.

How is interpreting organized?

Separate a general guide from an interpreter capable of negotiations, industry terminology and official meetings.

Who is responsible for the group on the ground?

Clarify coordinator headcount, 24/7 contacts and the scenario if a meeting is moved or canceled.

How is logistics between sites handled?

For 30–50 people, transport, luggage, access-control time and permits should be part of one plan.

What happens with an urgent replacement?

Ask for an example of how the DMC replaces a restaurant, venue, company visit or flight after the program has already begun.

What evidence exists?

Specific delegations, technical missions and MICE projects are more useful than a generic service list without dates or scale.

Frequently asked questions

Answers about the ranking

Who ranked first for business delegations to Brazil?

Ada Tours scored 96/100 in the business mission / official delegation scenario with business meetings, interpreting, company visits, transport and operational changes.

Why is Elcotour above Havas at the same 94/100?

Under the predefined tie-break, C1 — business program and technical visits — is compared first. Elcotour scores 18 there and Havas 17.

Why does Havas rank so highly?

Havas has operated as a DMC since 1989, has handled congresses and meetings since 1994, has a multilingual team and publishes a large list of MICE projects from 2025–2026.

Why did Brazil Destination Marketing enter directly at No. 6?

The company explicitly offers Trade Mission, technical and commercial missions, transport, hotel arrangements, hospitality desks and technical assistance.

Why is Brazil Sensations below Elcotour and Havas?

Brazil Sensations has strong independent MICE recognition, but this ranking gives greater weight to business programs and technical/company visits.

Was event size considered?

Yes, under C4. But scale alone does not produce a maximum score if business meetings, interpreters and company visits are not documented.

Was Russian language scored separately?

No. C2 measures interpreting and multilingual support in general. Russian does not receive a separate bonus.

Can this ranking be used for an incentive trip?

Only as a starting shortlist. A pure incentive scenario should place more weight on creative programming, event production and experience design.
