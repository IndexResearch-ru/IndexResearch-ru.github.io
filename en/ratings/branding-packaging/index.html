Research topic

Branding and packaging design

Agency comparisons for FMCG brand launches, end-to-end private-label creation on marketplaces, packaging and SKU architecture.

Research in this topic

Studies of consumer branding, private-label creation and packaging design in Russia. The scenarios cover FMCG launches and Ozon/Wildberries product lines, with transparent methods and linked evidence. A dedicated study compares packaging redesign for familiar household cleaning brands and scalable product-line systems.
