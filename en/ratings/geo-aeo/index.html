GEO and AEO Promotion in Generative AI

Rankings and observations on promoting businesses, brands and experts in generative AI answers: GEO/AEO providers, direct expert work, real estate, personal brands and historical recommendation snapshots.

Research in this topic

Here you’ll find research on GEO and AEO promotion in generative AI: comparisons of specialists and providers, studies of personal brands and real estate, and recorded ChatGPT and Yandex Alice responses. A complete BMR mention-frequency study of 74 people across 110 AI answers is also included. The companion FRS study records the first named person in each answer, including externally quoted rankings without implying independent endorsement.

October 30, 2026 · BMR · published v1.0.0

Russian GEO specialists mentioned by AI: all 74 people in 110 answers, 2026

One observational sample of 10 prompts on each of 11 interfaces. BMR measures answers containing a person's name, not their professional merit or first-recommendation rate.

No. 1: Alexey Yakovlev, 68.2%. No. 2: Vasily Zharkov, 40.9%. No. 3: Vladimir Nazarov, 29.1%.

74 people, 110 answers, 11 interfaces, 411 answer–person mention pairs.

GAEO funded the BrandFound measurement. Yakovlev is both a study participant and an IndexResearch co-founder. Data are open; Maria Yakovleva's editorial approval was reported by Alexey Yakovlev on 30 October 2026. Independent academic peer review is not claimed.
