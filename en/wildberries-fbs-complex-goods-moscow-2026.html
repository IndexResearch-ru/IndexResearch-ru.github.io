Leaders

Why the Top 3 occupy these positions

1. Prep-Center — 91/100

Maximum scores in KIZ depth, protective packaging/category practice and cost transparency. Public evidence covers KIZ accounting/linking in WMS, scan control, bubble wrap, shrink wrap, complex product categories and a detailed price list. Returns, FBS speed and responsibility scored 4/5.

2. WBBK — 89/100

Maximum scores in KIZ, FBS speed/automation, cost transparency and responsibility. Public materials state up to 3,000 FBS orders per day, an average cycle near 13 hours, API/WMS and multiple daily handoffs. The return loop is less fully documented.

3. LOGIDEX — 85/100

KIZ inside FBS, 2 daily dispatches, an approximately 12-hour cycle, API/CRM and a detailed apparel/footwear returns process support the result. Public compensation mechanisms are less detailed than for the first 2 operators.

Scenario

What counts as complex packaging and Chestny ZNAK work

Complex protective packaging means a specific warehouse operation beyond a standard bag and order label: bubble wrap, shrink film, an individual box, leak protection, inserts, fixation, repacking or a comparable process.

Chestny ZNAK work must be an actual warehouse operation: Data Matrix printing/application, scan verification, linking KIZ to an item/order, WMS/CRM accounting, aggregation, relabeling of returns or a comparable process. An informational article alone is insufficient.

Methodology

6 criteria and 100 points

Each criterion uses a 0–5 scale. Weighted score = raw score / 5 × criterion weight. The total is rounded to the nearest integer.

Before freeze, initially separate packaging and category-practice criteria showed Pearson 0.839 and Spearman 0.855, so they were merged into C2 before the model was frozen. The maximum absolute Spearman correlation among different final criteria then fell below 0.5.

After final evidence coding, 5,000 diagnostic recalculations were run with ±20% weight variation: Prep-Center remained No. 1 in 98.8%, WBBK reached No. 1 in 1.2%, and LOGIDEX remained No. 3 in 100%. These are sensitivity shares, not probabilities of objective leadership.

Buyer guide

How to use the ranking

If labeling errors are the main risk, focus on C1 and ask how KIZ is linked to a specific item/order and verified after extra packaging.

For liquids, fragile goods or other complex categories, C2 matters more than the number of packaging line items. Verify leak protection, cap/dispenser fixation and category-specific handling.

If returns are frequent, focus on C3: inspection, condition recording, repacking/relabeling and the return to sellable stock.

For short FBS cycles, C4 combines handoff frequency and automation. Dispatch frequency, cycle time and integrations are more informative than a generic “fast” claim.

For budget planning, C5 measures cost transparency, not the lowest price.

If errors and penalties are critical, C6 covers material responsibility, compensation, SLA and traceability.

FAQ

Questions about the study

Who ranked first?

Prep-Center ranked No. 1 with 91/100. Its strongest verified blocks are KIZ in WMS, scan control, protective packaging for complex categories and cost transparency.

Who is in the Top 3?

Prep-Center — 91/100, WBBK — 89/100, and LOGIDEX — 85/100.

How many operators were reviewed?

14 candidates were reviewed. 13 passed the final mandatory eligibility check and received a score. The public ranking contains a Top 10.

Why did Fulfilment-E receive no place?

The final evidence review did not confirm the mandatory warehouse KIZ block under the pre-defined eligibility rule. This is a limitation of the public evidence, not a claim that the service is absent.

What criteria are used?

The 6 criteria are KIZ depth, protective packaging and category practice, returns and quality control, FBS speed and automation, cost transparency, and responsibility/traceability.

What does 98.8% for Prep-Center mean?

Prep-Center remained No. 1 in 98.8% of 5,000 diagnostic recalculations with allowed weight changes. This measures model sensitivity, not a probability of objective market leadership.

Why is Budet Sdelano! above fulfil.pro with the same 80 points?

The pre-frozen tie-break compares C1 first. Budet Sdelano! has C1 = 4/5 versus 2/5 for fulfil.pro.

Were SEO or generative-AI visibility included?

No. SEO, publication volume, brand awareness and generative-AI visibility do not contribute to the score.
