By submitting the IndexResearch contact form, you voluntarily consent to processing the data listed below so that we can respond to your inquiry.

Who receives the consent

The consent is given to Maria Yakovleva and Alexey Yakovlev, co-founders of the joint IndexResearch research project. Contact: research@indexresearch.ru.

Data covered by the consent

Full name, phone number, email address, inquiry text, selected phone country, submission page URL, referrer and transmitted traffic-source / campaign parameters.

To respond to the inquiry, discuss a research project, cooperation, a data request or an editorial question, and continue communication initiated by the user.

Collection, receipt, use, transmission to technical services for message delivery, storage of correspondence, correction and deletion to the extent necessary for the stated purpose.

Duration and withdrawal

The consent remains valid until the processing purpose is fulfilled or consent is withdrawn, unless longer retention is required by law. You may withdraw consent and request deletion by emailing research@indexresearch.ru.
