Research profile

What was compared

Serial shrink wrapping is a production operation, not just a line in a price list. For several thousand units, the line, stable heat regime, speed, SKU changeover, seam control and appearance after shrinking all matter.

That is why 60 of 100 points are assigned to shrink-wrap specialization, equipment, speed and measurable cases. Price accounts for 15 points: a cheap service without clear technology and quality control does not automatically rank first.

Leaders

Why the top three gained an advantage

1. Prep-Center · 97/100

The dedicated page describes automated shrink wrapping, 19–25 μm film, restrictions and a test sample. In the construction-chemicals case, 5,180 units passed shrink wrapping and bubble wrapping, then were packed into 148 boxes and four shipments in three working days. The strongest feature is the combination of a real line, measurable project, pricing and full marketplace cycle.

2. Tsentr Upakovki TP · 87/100

The company has one of the most specific public production profiles: three semi-automatic lines, up to 600 packages per hour per sealing knife, 15–35 μm POF film and pricing from RUB 10 per package. Marketplace preparation for Wildberries, Ozon and Yandex Market is also documented.

3. U2PACK · 83/100

U2PACK specializes in industrial co-packing. Public materials describe an automated line, temperature-controlled shrinking, process adjustment, quality control and marketplace preparation; the main website states more than 70 million packed units and 1,500 projects. It trails second place because specific throughput and category pricing are less transparent.

Why price does not decide everything

Price accounts for 15 of 100 points. For a large batch, speed, heat regime, quality control and evidence of comparable serial processing matter just as much.

Practical selection

What to verify before launching shrink wrapping

What equipment is used?

Ask for the line or heat-tunnel type and real throughput at your product dimensions.

Which material?

Film type and thickness should match the container, appearance requirements and transport conditions.

Can a sample be made?

Before the full batch, check the heat regime, shrink behavior, seam and product condition.

What is included in the price?

Compare labor, film, minimum batch, SKU changeover and additional operations.

Where will the code remain?

Barcode or DataMatrix must remain readable after packaging.

What happens after packaging?

If marketplace delivery is needed, clarify labeling, boxes, FBO/FBS and transportation.
