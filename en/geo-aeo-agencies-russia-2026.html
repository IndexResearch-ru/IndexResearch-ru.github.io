GEO/AEO providers in Russia in 2026: public prices and service scope across 23 companies

Public price indications and GEO/AEO service scope across 23 Russian providers: which costs are monthly, what the provider does and which additional payments need verification.

No full three-month contractual estimate covering compulsory costs was verified for any examined offer. This is a limitation of public information, not a claim that the providers cannot supply the service.

Disclosure: GAEO.ru is owned by Alexey Yakovlev, an IndexResearch co-founder. The same source rules apply to all 23 providers.

What the price observations mean

From 36 initially screened candidates, 23 were selected for detailed review. The 59 observations include offers, extras, start-up phases and alternate indexed copies of the same page. They are not 59 distinct plans. A monthly unit is explicit in 30 observations, unspecified in 20, and other in 9. The RUB 150,000 monthly figure is an illustrative buyer budget, not an admission filter; higher-priced providers remain in the sample.

How the comparison was conducted

The evidence unit is a dated statement about a specific plan. An entry-level monitoring subscription is not equivalent to a recurring service that creates content and implements website changes. Optional extras are not treated as mandatory without proof; a first-month sprint is not multiplied into a three-month retainer. Unknown billing periods and older index snapshots are clearly labelled. No provider quality, AI visibility or performance scores are calculated.

IndexResearch methodology

Published terms for each company

Each profile separates the stated price, the service description and the limits of what can be inferred. Missing details are questions for the provider, not evidence that a service does not exist.

Demis Group

Public offer: Megatron: RUB 137,000 when the client writes the copy; turnkey RUB 152,000 (billing period not confirmed).

Demis Group describes Megatron as a standalone GEO offer. Other advertised packages depend on a separate SEO subscription and must not be priced as this service. Another RUB 95,900 figure appears without a sufficiently clear scope, so it cannot be treated as the full standalone GEO price. Confirm copywriting duties and the billing period before comparing quotes.

Official evidence: SRC-P11-01. See the canonical source register for the full URL and snapshot date.

Digital Geeks

Public offer: Start RUB 150,000/month; Advanced RUB 250,000/month.

Digital Geeks describes GEO work spanning website and content optimization, external placements and brand presence. The broad service description does not prove that every activity is included in the Start package. The number and type of placements, implementation responsibilities and any required fees need to be specified in a written proposal.

Official evidence: SRC-P09-01. See the canonical source register for the full URL and snapshot date.

Dojo Media

Public offer: Start from RUB 85,000; Optima from RUB 110,000; Large from RUB 180,000 (billing period not confirmed).

Dojo Media publishes GEO packages with different levels of ongoing support and SEO integration. The indexed official page does not reliably establish the billing period for each quoted amount or compulsory external placement costs. The figures are therefore published as price indications with an unresolved unit of payment.

Official evidence: SRC-P23-01. See the canonical source register for the full URL and snapshot date.

Evrica Marketing

Public offer: GEO promotion from RUB 90,790 (billing period unspecified).

Evrica Marketing describes an individually scoped GEO service addressing brand visibility and website optimization. To assess a recurring arrangement, a buyer would need confirmation of the billing period, required work and pricing for months two and three. The published starting figure alone does not establish a three-month budget.

Official evidence: SRC-P21-01. See the canonical source register for the full URL and snapshot date.

GAEO.ru / Alexey Yakovlev

Public offer: GEO promotion from RUB 79,900/month; GEO + content from RUB 99,900/month; financial-guarantee option from RUB 114,900/month.

GAEO's base package provides implementation specifications for the client's team. Under GEO + content, the provider creates content and coordinates publication. The guarantee-based service includes provider-side implementation of changes. Each extra 10 queries and each additional AI system beyond the base allocation costs RUB 10,000/month; the full budget and guarantee terms are contractual. GAEO is connected to an IndexResearch co-founder, as disclosed above.

Official evidence: SRC-P01-01. See the canonical source register for the full URL and snapshot date.

GEO Scout

Public offer: First-month sprint: RUB 90,000 or 140,000; platform subscription and separately ordered work thereafter.

GEO Scout presents month one as a managed sprint involving website, content, placements and measurement. Provider-side implementation of website changes is offered as an extra option from RUB 15,000.

Official evidence: SRC-P06-01. See the canonical source register for the full URL and snapshot date.

GEOSEO Agency

GEOSEO Agency differentiates packages by the depth of technical work and volume of materials. Start includes one publication and two briefs, whereas Optimum specifies two to three external placements monthly. The placement allowance in Optimum cannot be assumed for the less expensive Start package.

Official evidence: SRC-P13-01. See the canonical source register for the full URL and snapshot date.

PRIMO Agency

PRIMO Agency separates monitoring and recommendations from active content and placement work, and then from a wider PR-supported team package. РА Ковалевы (Kovalevy Advertising Agency)

Under the lower-priced Management plan, the client writes the materials while the agency issues tasks and organizes placements. Under Basic, the agency produces 10 texts. Reaspekt separates the initial preparation stage from later development, with several levels of ongoing work. These stage-specific amounts should not be presented as one ordinary monthly rate. The aggregate cost depends on the selected development level and additional contractual terms.

What to request before signing

Request a written plan and price for month 1, month 2 and month 3 separately. Confirm who writes content, implements website changes and pays for external placements; ask about VAT, mandatory third-party fees, measurement questions, report frequency and the person responsible for delivery.

Frequently asked questions

What is the lowest GEO price in the comparison?

Published offers start at RUB 30,000/month, but that is PRIMO Agency's monitoring-level package. Other figures relate to partial services, one-off sprints or prices described as starting points. The lowest verified price for complete three-month managed GEO cannot be established from the public evidence.

Can a business obtain a provider for under RUB 150,000 a month?

Some published monthly prices are below that amount. However, no complete contractual estimate for all required work and payments in each of the first three months has been verified. The RUB 150,000 figure is a buyer scenario, not a filter excluding providers from this documentary study.

Why are the companies not sorted by price?

The figures refer to different products: monitoring, one-off sprints, managed services, SEO bundled with GEO, and separately priced tasks. Some do not even specify a billing period. Sorting them from cheapest to most expensive would suggest comparability that has not been demonstrated.

Do external publications come with the quoted package?

It depends on the specific plan. Some companies state quantities of content and placements, while others quote external strategy separately or determine media costs by contract. Ask about the number and type of platforms and any compulsory third-party expenditure.

Who writes the content and implements website changes?

Responsibilities vary. For example, GAEO's base option gives tasks to the client's team, while a higher-tier package includes provider-produced content. With Kovalevy Advertising Agency, the client writes material in Management, while the agency produces texts in Basic. The same distinction needs to be checked for every offer.

Why is there no three-month total?

None of the examined offers supplies a verified complete three-month estimate covering all mandatory charges and time periods. Multiplying a headline starting price by three would manufacture an unsupported total.

Why does this study not rank provider quality?

It compares disclosed commercial terms, not delivered results. A well-documented price page is not proof that a provider's service is more effective. A quality ranking would require a different assessment design and independent performance evidence.

How are older search-index snapshots treated?

Their age is recorded in the source register. An indexed copy can show what appeared on a provider's official page at a particular point, but it does not establish that the same price is available at the time of reading.

Data, sources and limitations

Official supplier pages evidence advertised terms, not service performance. Some pages were available only through older indexed copies. The study does not identify a cheapest fully managed provider and does not present a hypothetical three-month invoice as a verified total.
