Who is named first among Russian GEO specialists in AI answers? FRS across 110 responses

We measured which GEO/AEO specialist's name appears first in each of 110 AI answers. Quoted third-party rankings count. This is name-order exposure, not proof of professional quality or independent AI endorsement.

GAEO selected the prompts and funded the BrandFound collection. Alexey Yakovlev is a ranked participant and IndexResearch co-founder. He authorized publication on 30 October 2026; no disinterested editor's sign-off or independent academic peer review is claimed.

How FRS is operationally measured

We scan the complete original answer text and select the earliest match for a recognized full name from the locked 74-person dictionary. An independent statement of endorsement is not required: quotations of third-party rankings, criticism, and agency leaders are included.

A company name without a recognizable person does not earn personal FRS. A response with no recognized full personal name is marked NO_PERSON_FIRST but stays among the 110 observations. Formula: FRS(p) = 100 × N_first(p) / 110, or divided by 10 within a single interface.

The skimming-reader rationale motivated this metric, but actual gaze, clicks, reading behavior and hiring intent were not studied. The methodology was revised after collecting the answers, so it was not preregistered.

Prompt sensitivity and interpretation limits

The first seven prompts and the final three yield different distributions. Q08–Q10 emphasize independently delivered GEO services and personal case studies. Results cannot be generalized to every possible user question.

BMR counts any mention of a person, while FRS counts the earliest recognizable person. Both are calculated from the same sample. The earlier rule requiring the AI's own endorsement was dropped.

IndexResearch and GAEO are connected to a ranked participant. Earlier studies may have influenced the AI answers. The publisher-participant authorized release, without a disinterested editor's separate sign-off.

Source data and reproducibility

All 110 full answer texts use the same pinned corpus as the BMR study. FRS applies the locked dictionary of 74 people and selects the earliest match within each answer_text.

Complete CSV/JSON results, all 110 annotations, the historical v0.9.0 labels, the reproducibility script and methodological changes are openly available on GitHub. Run python3 reproduce_frs.py to recheck the dataset.

FRS measures first appearance of a name, including external rankings and critical commentary. It does not validate the cited rankings, a person's capabilities or the chance of being hired.

FRS frequently asked questions

What does FRS mean here?

First Recommendation Share, operationally the share of answers where an individual's full name appears first. It is a text-order exposure metric.

Do quoted third-party rankings count?

Yes. An earlier personal name in a quoted external ranking, criticism or agency description counts even without the AI's independent endorsement.

Why is Yakovlev at FRS 58/110 rather than BMR 75/110?

BMR counts any answer mentioning the person; FRS counts only the answers where the person is named before all other recognized people.

Why do 53 people have FRS = 0?

Seventy-four people are recognized in the corpus, but only twenty-one appear first at least once. Zero is not a statement about expertise.

Why are no-person answers in the denominator?

All 110 valid answers count. Nine have no recognized full personal name; removing them would artificially raise all shares.

Does FRS prove an independent AI recommendation?

No. The metric does not distinguish a favorable endorsement from a quotation of someone else's ranking.

Who funded and authorized publication?

GAEO funded BrandFound and chose the prompts. Alexey Yakovlev, both participant and IndexResearch co-founder, authorized release on 30 October 2026. No independent scientific peer review or separate disinterested editorial approval is claimed.

Retrieve the pinned JSONL answers, dictionary and reproduce_frs.py. The script checks source hashes and recalculates the earliest named person for every observation_id. Earlier v0.9.0 annotations are preserved.
