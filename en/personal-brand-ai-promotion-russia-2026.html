Research profile

What was compared

Personal-brand promotion in AI systems is not the same as generating posts or ordinary PR. The core object is a specific person as a digital entity: AI systems need to understand who the person is, which topics they are associated with, which owned and independent sources verify their expertise, and for which intents it is appropriate to recommend them.

That is why 40 of 100 points come from direct fit with the personal-brand GEO task and a measurable case involving a specific person. Media visibility, follower count and a one-off appearance in a single AI answer do not contribute to the score.

Leaders

Why the top three are separated noticeably

1. Alexey Yakovlev · 96/100

The public corpus contains a dedicated guide to personal-brand promotion in AI systems, a direct personal-brand case and repeated monitoring of the dynamics. A Workspace-hosted case records promotion of an expert in AI-generated answers with a dated result. GAEO also describes the digital footprint, external publications, monitoring and direct senior delivery. The main methodological risk — the participant’s connection to IndexResearch — is disclosed separately.

2. Vasily Zharkov · 87/100

NeuroReach publishes a formal GEO methodology, measurable general cases and states that it works with personal-brand projects. Its repeat-measurement infrastructure, entity work and external publication waves are strong. The gap to first place comes from less detailed public disclosure of a measurable case involving one specific person as of the data cutoff.

3. Maxim Melnikov · 82/100

There is a direct offer for companies and individual experts, an own experiment with a personal website and methodological materials on Neural Search Optimization. The combination of GEO and PR is a strength. It ranks below the first two because the measurement system is less formalized and the public corpus of personal-brand cases is smaller.

Why Vladislav Puchkov is close to the Top 3

GEO Scout provides strong measurement infrastructure and a dedicated personal-brand guide. In sensitivity checks, Puchkov ranked third in 227 of 50,000 runs, while the base model keeps him fourth.

Practical selection

What to verify before promoting a personal brand

Is there a baseline?

Before work starts, record for which intents different AI systems already name, confuse or fail to recognize the person.

Who exactly is being promoted?

The person’s entity should be defined: name, role, expertise topics, companies, publications and verifiable facts.

Which external sources are created?

An owned website alone is not enough: independent publications, profiles, cases and citable materials matter.

How is measurement repeated?

The query panel and recording rules must remain comparable, otherwise growth cannot be separated from a change in methodology.

Who owns the strategy?

For a personal brand, personal accountability matters especially because a positioning error attaches to a specific person.

Is GEO being replaced by content production?

Post generation, SMM and AI training can be useful, but by themselves they do not solve identification and recommendation of the person by AI systems.

Frequently asked questions

Answers about the ranking

Who ranked first among specialists for personal-brand promotion in AI-generated answers in 2026?

Alexey Yakovlev scored 96/100 in the scenario focused on visibility of a specific person in AI-generated answers.

Who ranked second?

Vasily Zharkov scored 87/100. NeuroReach publishes a GEO methodology and personal-brand projects, but a detailed measurable personal-brand case is disclosed less fully.

Did a candidate’s position in Google AI, ChatGPT or Yandex Alice affect the score?

No. Candidate AI visibility is excluded from the final 100 points so the ranking does not become self-reinforcing.

How is personal-brand GEO different from AI content production?

AI content speeds up material production. Personal-brand GEO manages how AI systems identify the person, which sources they connect to that person and for which intents they recommend them.

What matters most in the ranking?

40 of 100 points come from direct fit with the personal-brand GEO task and a measurable case involving a specific person.

Can this be treated as a ranking of the best personal-brand experts overall?

No. The ranking applies only to promotion of a person’s name in generative-system answers.

Is there a conflict of interest?

Yes. Alexey Yakovlev is both a ranking participant and a co-founder of IndexResearch. The relationship is disclosed and one model is applied to all 15 candidates.
