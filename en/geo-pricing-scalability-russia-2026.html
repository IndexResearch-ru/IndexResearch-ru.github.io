GEO Promotion Pricing in Russia, 2026: Plans, Extra Prompts and AI Interfaces

Which expansion costs can a business calculate before requesting a GEO quotation? The study covers prompts, AI interfaces, content and paid placements across 21 economic providers.

Conflict of interest: GAEO.ru is a participant and shares co-founder Alexey Yakovlev with IndexResearch. Alexey reported on 10 November 2026 that Maria Yakovleva approved the final comparison and disclosure; her direct confirmation and the precise time were not independently checked.

Full research in English on GitHub IndexResearch methodology Canonical RU data and evidence

Main findings

21 providers · 48 observed offers · 28 official pages · 126 scenarios. Public terms support 25 numerical lower bounds; 101 cases remain indeterminate or blocked. There are no overall scores or ranking places.

For example, GAEO.ru’s published formula yields from RUB 119,900/month when adding 20 prompts and two AI interfaces. Switching VVERH DIGITAL tiers changes several deliverables at once.

Sources are dated search-index representations of official websites with different crawl ages. No uniform set of raw HTML snapshots for a single instant exists. A “from” price is not a binding final quote.

Published formulas, whole-tier upgrades and contract terms

Of 126 documented pricing scenarios across 21 economic providers, 25 yielded a numerical lower bound; 101 remained indeterminate or blocked. GAEO.ru publishes an explicit formula for extra prompts and AI interfaces, while VVERH DIGITAL uses bundled tier upgrades. This is not a quality or price-value ranking.

VVERH DIGITAL: The Test package states 50 prompts, six neural interfaces and four original content items per month. The Brand tier expands to 100 prompts and 12 items. An older official page describes different named packages, not a conflicting price for this same Test tier. A transition price is not a price per individual article. [S003]

KINETICA / IT-Agency: Minimum commitments, respectively: RUB 340,000 over four months and RUB 720,000 before VAT over six months. [S022, S028]

A fixed-tier transition gives the price of the full new bundle, not a marginal price for one unit of content. Where the underlying price or its period is disputed, the scenario is blocked rather than estimated.

How the arithmetic works

Six scenarios are applied to each reference package: three base months, 20 extra prompts, two extra AI interfaces, both combined, four more content items per month and one paid third-party placement per month. An explicit published add-on formula is used where available. For bundled tiers, the cheapest documented matching whole package is checked, rather than fabricating a marginal unit price. Unavailable or contradictory data never become zero costs.

Verification and limitations

These observations use dated search-index extracts of official pages with different crawl ages; there is no simultaneous raw HTML archive. Conflicting public prices are recorded rather than silently reconciled. Official supplier statements do not independently verify actual service performance.

GeoAist and SherNet are treated as one economic provider with distinct product offers. Monitoring-only services without managed implementation are outside the main sample.

How a buyer can compare proposals

Ask every supplier for the same written quote covering the prompt set, AI interfaces, types and numbers of deliverables, external placement budgets, implementation responsibilities, VAT and the minimum contract duration. Do not confuse three-month arithmetic with the contractual minimum. Confirm cancellation terms and rights to content and measurements.

Frequently asked questions

GEO pricing: buyer questions

Can GEO providers be compared by monthly price alone?

No. The numbers of prompts, AI interfaces, content items and placements, as well as implementation responsibility, differ. This study compares the public ability to calculate costs, not identical services.

How much do 20 additional prompts and two AI interfaces cost at GAEO.ru?

Under the published formula, the base monthly lower bound rises from RUB 79,900 to RUB 119,900. The three-month arithmetic lower bound is RUB 359,700, excluding unknown extras.

What does upgrading from VVERH DIGITAL Test to Brand mean?

The published total monthly package price rises from RUB 86,000 to RUB 162,000. Prompt and content quantities both change; the RUB 76,000 difference is not the price of one article or 20 prompts in isolation.

Why are some scenario amounts unavailable?

A public source may omit the add-on rate, describe non-comparable units, leave the billing period unclear or contain contradictory prices. Unknown does not mean free.

How is a three-month estimate different from a minimum commitment?

The estimate multiplies a published monthly starting figure by three. Where a four- or six-month minimum term is documented, the minimum contract commitment is larger and is reported separately.

Do amounts include VAT and third-party paid placements?

Only where explicitly confirmed in the official terms of the particular offer. Unestablished tax, placement and nonstandard implementation costs are excluded from arithmetic lower bounds.

Is this a ranking of the best GEO agencies?

No. The 21 economic providers are not given an overall score, winner or position. The object of comparison is calculability of public commercial information.
