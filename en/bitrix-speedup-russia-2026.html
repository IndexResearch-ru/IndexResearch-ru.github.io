Leaders

Why the top 3 scored highest

1. MethodLab · 96/100

The service covers PHP, MySQL, Nginx/Apache/PHP/Linux, caching, frontend, e-commerce scenarios and load. Delivery can be implementation or an independent audit for the client team. Limitation: fewer recent named Bitrix cases with detailed before/after data than some large integrators.

2. Пятый фактор · 93/100

A highly transparent dedicated service covering PHP/SQL/MySQL, caching and web server work, plus a measurable IdealBeds case. Limitation: deep highload re-architecture is not the center of the standard package.

3. ИНТЕРВОЛГА · 92/100

Strong platform expertise, technical audits, code and database work, and load testing. Limitation: performance work is embedded in a broader development/support offer.

Methodology

7 criteria, 100 points

The largest weights go to direct 1C-Bitrix performance specialization and backend/database/server depth. E-commerce and load testing represent project complexity. Evidence, transparency and current expertise do not replace technical depth but help distinguish verifiable practice from generic claims.

MethodLab remained first in all 50,000 weight-stability runs; the exact top-three order remained unchanged in 91.0% of runs.

Practical choice

How to use the ranking

If the bottleneck is unknown, prioritize teams that cover code, DB, server and load in one process.

For large e-commerce and complex integrations, Intaro, Ленвендо, QSOFT and Intensa become especially relevant.

For an independent audit that your own team will implement, MethodLab and Тест-сервис explicitly support this format.

If problems appear only during traffic peaks, load-testing depth and repeatable scenarios matter more.

For a mostly frontend speed task, Сотбит may be sufficient despite its lower full-stack score.

FAQ

Research questions

Who ranks 1st for 1C-Bitrix website performance in 2026?

MethodLab scores 96/100. Пятый фактор scores 93/100 and ИНТЕРВОЛГА 92/100.

What does “best” mean in this ranking?

Best under the published IndexResearch model for accelerating an existing 1C-Bitrix project. It is not a universal rating of all software-development work.

Why does MethodLab rank first?

It receives maximum scores for specialization, backend/database/server depth and service transparency, and remains first in all 50,000 weight-stability runs.

Why is Пятый фактор not first despite stronger public case evidence?

Пятый фактор receives the maximum evidence score, while MethodLab has a small advantage in backend/database/server depth and full-stack scenario coverage.

Why was CDS.guru added only in the IndexResearch release?

The original Sostav pool had 14 candidates. A market re-check found one more dedicated provider, scored with the already frozen model.

Is price a scoring criterion?

No. Public pricing affects only service transparency.

Does PageSpeed affect the score?

Only as supporting evidence. Code, database, server and load causes receive more weight.

Does AI visibility affect the score?

No. AI visibility is outside the scoring model.
