How to arrange an Antarctica cruise from Ushuaia

Three purchase models, 15 documented providers

Check physical embarkation dates separately from the start of the tour package. Confirm whether each hotel, domestic flight and transfer is included in the exact booked fare. A Russian-language website does not prove Russian-speaking crew onboard. Landings depend on weather and ice.

28 companies examined; 15 selected; 34 source records; 101 mapped factual claims. No ranks or numeric scores.

Booking checklist

What to confirm before paying

Check physical embarkation dates separately from the start of the tour package. Confirm whether each hotel, domestic flight and transfer is included in the exact booked fare. A Russian-language website does not prove Russian-speaking crew onboard. Landings depend on weather and ice.

A Russian-language site, a Russian-speaking sales contact, a Russian-language group and Russian-speaking ship staff are separate promises. Not confirmed is not proof a service is unavailable.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this a ranking of Antarctica companies?

No. It is an editorial selection of 15 documented examples from 28 candidates without ranks or numerical scores.

Where do the selected expeditions depart?

The cited programs involve physical embarkation in Ushuaia; some tour packages begin a day earlier.

Are flights included?

Some stated packages include a charter or domestic flight, others explicitly exclude flights or do not confirm them.

Does the hotel come with the cruise?

Hotel inclusion is specific to the cited fare and sailing, and should be confirmed before payment.

Are transfers always included?

No. Each transfer segment may have different conditions.

Can I expect Russian-speaking guides onboard?

A Russian-language seller or webpage does not prove a Russian-speaking expedition team.

Why are Intrepid and Polar Latitudes both listed?

They are separate commercial participants associated with an Ocean Albatros voyage.

Does Ada Tours operate Hondius?

The available evidence does not establish that the cited Ada Tours offer is on Hondius.

Are Antarctic landings guaranteed?

No. All shore landings and Zodiac activities depend on safety, weather and ice.

Where is the underlying evidence?
