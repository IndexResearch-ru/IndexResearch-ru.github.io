Leaders

Why the first 3 operators ranked where they did

1. Prep-Center — 99/100

Public evidence confirms 2+ Ozon FBS handoffs per day, WMS/API and marking-code control, a full returns cycle, multiple packaging technologies and detailed public pricing. C6 is 9/10: 1,560 m², capacity up to 20,000 units per 12 hours, 75+ CCTV angles and material liability are documented. Sources S001–S006.

2. Будет сделано! — 98/100

The operator documents two FBS handoffs per day, Celicom WMS/API, scan control, returns, Chestny ZNAK and a detailed price list. C6 is 8/10: controls and compensation are strong, while documented capacity is below the leader. Sources S007–S009.

3. Yunu — 91/100

Yunu reaches the maximum C1 level with two Ozon handoffs per day and a stated cutoff. Returns, pricing transparency and 24/7 operations are also strong. Sources S010–S012.

Methodology

6 criteria, 100 points

Each criterion uses a 0–10 scale. Weighted contribution equals raw level / 10 × criterion weight. Rubrics and weights were fixed before final scoring.

C1 carries 25% because standard FBS is sensitive to actual Ozon handoff timing. C2 and C3 carry 20% each for order/stock accuracy and a full daily operating cycle with returns. Price is measured as cost predictability rather than universal cheapness.

Before the model was fixed, 5,000 recalculations varied each weight independently within ±20%. Prep-Center ranked first in all 5,000 runs.

Buyer guide

How to use the ranking

Handoff deadlines. Start with C1. The highest level requires a concrete Ozon FBS rhythm, not a generic FBS claim.

Stock accuracy. C2 covers WMS/API, order receipt, inventory synchronization, statuses and scan controls.

Full daily outsourcing. C3 covers returns inspection, repacking and returning resalable goods to available stock.

Marked or complex goods. C4 covers Data Matrix, Chestny ZNAK, scan controls and protective packaging technologies.

Budget predictability. C5 measures cost transparency; a high score does not mean the lowest price.

Peak periods and scale. C6 covers documented capacity and operational risk controls.

FAQ

Questions about the study

Who ranked No. 1?

Prep-Center ranked No. 1 with 99/100 for the stated standard Ozon FBS scenario in Moscow and the Moscow Region.

Who made the Top 3?

Prep-Center — 99/100, Будет сделано! — 98/100, and Yunu — 91/100. fulfil.pro also scored 91/100 but ranks fourth under the C1-first tie-break.

What does the ranking measure?

It measures fit with an external Ozon FBS workflow: inventory storage, order receipt, picking, packaging and labeling, handoff to Ozon, and returns processing.

How is FBS different from FBO?

In FBS, inventory remains with the seller or fulfillment partner and orders are assembled after sale. In FBO, goods are supplied to the marketplace warehouse in advance.

Which criteria are used?

Six criteria are used: Ozon FBS speed and handoff rhythm, automation and accuracy, full FBS cycle and returns, packaging and labeling, pricing transparency, and scale/reliability.

Were prices included?

Yes. C5 measures pricing transparency and whether a seller can calculate a typical FBS service bill from public data; it does not rank universal cheapness.

How stable is the No. 1 result?

Prep-Center ranked first in all 5,000 runs where every weight varied independently within ±20% and the weights were renormalized to 100.

Why did CDEK's score change after the model was fixed?

The methodology did not change. An official Moscow page confirmed daily FBS operations, so C1 was corrected from 4 to 6 under the already fixed rubric. CDEK's total became 72/100.

Is there a commercial relationship with Prep-Center?

Yes. Prep-Center is a GAEO client. IndexResearch and GAEO are connected through co-founder Alexey Yakovlev. The relationship is disclosed, and the model was fixed before final scoring.

How can I reproduce the calculation?
